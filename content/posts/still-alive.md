It's been a long time since I wrote anything here. The last post is from years ago, and for so many reasons, 
I stopped for a long time. Hopefully that will change! I like writing! (sort of)

I have a bad habit of making a thing, and then never telling anyone about it. A bunch of these are LLM-assisted (thanks claude), and (almost exclusively) fueled by an idea from one or both of my kiddos. It's been super fun to watch LLMs get better, and at the same time watch my kiddos ideas improve. Listed in roughly reverse chronological order.

## Games

- **[Armageddon](/armageddon/)** — (Made with older kiddo) A first-person shooter about surviving the apocalypse and rebuilding society.
  Works with a Switch Pro Controller. 
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/armageddon.jpg" link="/armageddon/" alt="Armageddon title screen" >}}
- **[Limited Gravity](/limited-gravity/)** — (Made with younger kiddo) You are an alien. Space is yours to explore. Humans want you dead, other
  aliens want your help, and the missions that pay best are the ones most likely to kill you. 
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/limited-gravity.jpg" link="/limited-gravity/" alt="Limited Gravity alien creator" >}}
- **[Koala Nightmares](/koala-nightmares/)** — (Made with younger kiddo) A first-person horror game. You're house-sitting, you go camping in
  the jungle, and then it gets dark. The koalas are not friendly.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/koala-nightmares.jpg" link="/koala-nightmares/" alt="Koala Nightmares title screen" >}}
- **[Poop Monkey](/poop-monkey/)** — (Made with older kiddo) You are a monkey in a zoo. At night you pick the lock, steal
  food, and dodge Janitor Ron.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/poop-monkey.jpg" link="/poop-monkey/" alt="Poop Monkey start screen" >}}
- **[Dog Salon](/dog-salon/)** — (Made with younger kiddo) A grooming salon for dogs. You're the German Shepherd who runs it,
  dachshunds come in with very specific requests, and you never get to pick your tools. Sometimes it's soap. Sometimes
  it's a jar of bees. ¯\\\_(ツ)_/¯
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/dog-salon.jpg" link="/dog-salon/" alt="Salon Teckel opening screen" >}}
- **[Zoo Problems](/zoo-problems/)** — (Made with younger kiddo) A stealth comedy game built with Phaser. You're a giraffe sneaking around the
  zoo at night, collecting beauty products and avoiding guards with flashlights.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/zoo-problems.jpg" link="/zoo-problems/" alt="Zoo Problems title screen" >}}
- **[Wizard Barber](/wizard-barber/)** — (Made with older kiddo) Cut the beards of wizards. Wizards have curses. Upgrade your tools and try
  not to get cursed.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/wizard-barber.jpg" link="/wizard-barber/" alt="Wizard Barber title screen" >}}
- **[CapyDash](/capydash/)** — (Made with older kiddo) A Geometry Dash knockoff starring a capybara.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/capydash.jpg" link="/capydash/" alt="CapyDash welcome screen" >}}
- **[Capybara Clicker](/capybara-clicker/)** — (Made with older kiddo) A cookie clicker, but the cookie is a capybara and the upgrades are
  oranges. There's a leaderboard.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/capybara-clicker.jpg" link="/capybara-clicker/" alt="Capybara Clicker" >}}
- **[CapyKarma](/capykarma/)** — (Made without kiddo help) A karmic journey. You're a creature, you make choices, you die, you get reincarnated
  as something better or worse. It has anti-cheat, which tells you how the playtesting went.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/capykarma.jpg" link="/capykarma/" alt="CapyKarma, already dead" >}}

## Tools and experiments

- **[Mic Off](https://github.com/stringerbell/mic-off/releases)** — _Actually_ turn your mic off. It bugs me that this isn't the default.
- **[Icebreakers](/icebreakers/)** — Short on ideas? A pile of icebreaker questions for team meetings. (Made in rage/grief after a real-life office icebreaker.)
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/icebreakers.jpg" link="/icebreakers/" alt="Icebreakers" >}}
- **[Minimalist Meeting Notes](/ml-meeting-notes/)** — Use machine learning to pull the important parts out of your
  meeting notes. I [wrote about this one](/posts/using-machine-learning-to-summarize-meeting-notes/) back in 2018. This may or may have not been a premonition about the state of things now. This also got me at least one call from a recruiter. (lol.)
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/ml-meeting-notes.jpg" link="/ml-meeting-notes/" alt="Minimalist Meeting Notes" >}}
- **[Everybody Needs Therapy](/everybody-needs-therapy/)** — A poem I made with my brother.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/everybody-needs-therapy.jpg" link="/everybody-needs-therapy/" alt="Everybody Needs Therapy" >}}
- **[Probably Jesus](/wj/)** — Things Jesus *probably* said. May or may not be Trump quotes.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/wj.jpg" link="/wj/" alt="Things Jesus Probably Said" >}}
- **[This Jesus Does Not Exist](/thisjesusdoesnotexist/)** — Not imagined by a GAN. Also none of these pictures are of
  Jesus.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/thisjesusdoesnotexist.jpg" link="/thisjesusdoesnotexist/" alt="This Jesus Does Not Exist" >}}
- **[New Music Archive](/new-music-archive/)** — (abandoned. I don't really listen to music these days) Apple's "new music" list, archived every week. I got tired of
  interesting releases scrolling off the bottom of the page before I could listen to them.
  {{< figure src="/images/still-alive/new-music-archive.jpg" link="/new-music-archive/" alt="New Music Archive" >}}
